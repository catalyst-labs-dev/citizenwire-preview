 COMMENT:                           Supervisors Connolly and Holm asked about food rescue at River Place.
                                    Polk County Children, Family & Youth Services Director Eric Kool
                                    discussed the breakdown of food rescue during the pandemic. Kool noted
                                    that DMARC and Caring Hands both provide food rescue now, with Caring
                                    Hands currently providing more ready-to-eat options. Supervisor Connolly
                                    asked about poundage and procurement. Jodi Urich, CEO of Caring Hands
                                    Outreach Shelter, made comments in response. Supervisor Hockensmith
                                    asked about the application process for this project. County Administrator
                                    Frank Marasco replied that this project is outside of the grant process and
                                    noted that this model will be self-sustainable.. Supervisor Holm discussed
                                    food deserts outside of Des Moines. Supervisor Connolly emphasized the
                                    importance of collaboration with organizations in the food insecurity
                                    network. Supervisor Connolly proposed an amendment, which she later
                                    withdrew, to increase funding to DMARC and Food Bank of Iowa.
