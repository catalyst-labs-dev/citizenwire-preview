      PROCEEDINGS OF THE POLK COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
 The Polk County Board of Supervisors met in special session Monday, August 31, 2026 at 10:30
 a.m.

 MEMBERS PRESENT: Jill Altringer, Tom Hockensmith, Angela Connolly, Mark Holm, Matt
 McCoy.

 PUBLIC COMMENTS:           Carol Maher of downtown Des Moines made comments in regards to
                            bicycle storage at Prairie Meadows.

 COMMENTS:                  County Administrator Frank Marasco and Supervisors Hockensmith, Holm,
                            Altringer, Connolly, and McCoy made comments in regards to the Lease
                            Agreement with Prairie Meadows.

 RESOLUTION:                Resolution approving Agreement with Prairie Meadows.

                            VOTE YEA: Altringer, Hockensmith, Connolly, Holm, McCoy.


Moved by Altringer, Seconded by Holm to adjourn until September 8, 2026 at 10:30 a.m.
VOTE YEA: Altringer, Hockensmith, Connolly, Holm, McCoy.
