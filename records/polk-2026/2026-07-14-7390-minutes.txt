In the event of a tie, the election is determined by lot pursuant to Iowa Code Section 50.44. Names of the tied
candidates (Corey Walker-Zepeda and Jacob Dingman) were written on separate, uniform slips of paper. The
slips were folded and placed into a receptacle so that names were not visible to participants or observers. In
open session, Canvass Chairperson McCoy reached into the receptacle and drew one slip. The name drawn
was Jason Dingman who was then declared elected by Canvass Chairperson McCoy.

                      WE THEREFORE DECLARE: Jacob Dingman was duly elected.
